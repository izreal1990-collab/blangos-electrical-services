FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers about our service areas, estimates, turnaround times, and electrical solutions.

What areas in Ohio do you service? +

We work throughout Columbus, Ohio and surrounding communities within a 50-mile radius. This includes Columbus, Dublin, Grove City, Hilliard, Westerville, Upper Arlington, Gahanna, Reynoldsburg, Pickerington, Delaware, and nearby Central Ohio areas.

How do I get a free estimate or schedule service? +

You can book an appointment directly through our online Calendly scheduler below, submit our contact form with your project details, or call us directly at (614) 570-4434. We provide upfront, transparent estimates before starting any job.

What types of electrical services do you offer? +

We provide full residential and light commercial electrical services, including panel upgrades (100A to 200A service), EV charger installation (Level 2 home chargers), interior and exterior lighting installation, troubleshooting power loss or tripping breakers, outlet and switch replacement, ceiling fan wiring, and safety inspections.

How quickly can you respond for urgent repairs? +

For urgent electrical problems in the Columbus area (such as total power loss, sparking outlets, or burning smells), we provide rapid response. Call our direct line at (614) 570-4434 for immediate assistance.

What should I do if an outlet sparks or a circuit breaker trips repeatedly? +

If an outlet sparks or smells burnt, immediately turn off the circuit breaker that powers that area and do not plug anything into it. If a breaker trips repeatedly, do not force it back on—this indicates an overload, short circuit, or failing component. Call us at (614) 570-4434 so we can safely diagnose and resolve the issue.
